About AutoWorld

AutoWorld was founded with one simple goal: to connect passionate drivers with the cars of their dreams. We believe in quality, transparency, and top-tier customer service.

Since opening our first showroom in Astana, we have delivered over 1,500 inspected vehicles across Kazakhstan. Our certified technicians test every engine, battery pack, and safety system so our clients drive away with complete confidence.
